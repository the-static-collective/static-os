## Photo framing (optional)

Photograph **external** model/power labels, controls and ports of one or two candidate devices. Cover serial numbers, MAC addresses, activation codes, passphrases, private contact names and other identifying data. Do not open mains-powered equipment for a photograph.

## Promotion rule

A passport may move from UNINSPECTED to INSPECTED_UNPOWERED when the external label, leads, case and battery are visually checked. A working physical sensor/messenger requires a separate actual demonstration, and an independent physical-compute claim requires quantitative controlled input/output evidence.

*The pile is a field of possibilities, not yet an inventory of admitted instruments.*
