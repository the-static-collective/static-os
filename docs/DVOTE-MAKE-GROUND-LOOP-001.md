## Human hinge

The adapter preserves all three dispositions.

- `TAKE` + non-empty witness → eligible for NAV world-contact.
- `TAKE` + empty witness → `awaiting_witness`.
- `HOLD` → `held`.
- `PASS` → `passed`.

Only the first state may enter the executable loop.

```text
HOLD != FAILURE
PASS != FAILURE
SELECTION != WORLD CONTACT
```

## Canonical contradiction

The durable TAKE fixture reports that drawing an ordinary problem as a timeline exposed a new question.

A separate direct-observation fixture reports that a bounded representation-change trial produced no new question.

WORLD therefore preserves an independent contradiction rather than averaging the reports into a conclusion.

MAKE GROUND then changes the substrate: a temporary dual-run comparison card makes conditions, source paths, and the disagreement itself easier to compare.

The resulting comparison worksheet re-enters WITNESS as generated-downstream evidence. WORLD explicitly refuses to count it as a new independent witness.

Dogram receipts the complete path. NAV receives a proposed new heading.

## Edition return

The final object is `static.edition-return/v0`.

It carries:

- the original DVOTE receipt fingerprint;
- human disposition and witness;
- the field fertility delta;
- the path-preserving NAV reorientation;
- the laws that survived;
- one editorial question for the next MAKE GROUND edition.

It sets:

```json
"automatic_book_edit": false
```

The return is material for an editor, not authority over the book.
